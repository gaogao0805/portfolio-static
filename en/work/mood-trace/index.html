PRODUCT INTRO

What is bipolar disorder?

Bipolar disorder is a mental health condition that seriously affects emotional stability and calls for intervention and support. Over 8.4 million people live with it — on a high one moment, sinking into depression the next.

This project pairs a mobile app with a smart wristband to help people with bipolar disorder manage their emotions.

Mainstream mood-tracking and healing products cluster around two paths: one, represented by StressWatch and AMood, emphasizes biometric-driven stress monitoring; the other, such as MOODA, 心光 and 林间聊愈室, focuses on emotional expression, visualization, and AI companionship. Products like 解压小橙子 and Grow offer lightweight stress-relief or habit-building tools, but lack deeper emotional understanding.

Overall, the market still lacks a product that combines a ritual of emotional expression, intelligent trend insight, and actionable healing exercises.

Product Direction

Product direction

Building on user personas from earlier research, needs are distilled through Maslow's hierarchy and the Kano model to set the product's direction.

Delighters

  • Immersive healing interactions (tasks, guided breathing, meditation)
  • Journey-like experience (grow like a game)
  • AI companion (proactive reminders & insight)

Must-haves

  • Mood logging (journal + check-in)
  • History curves & data review
  • Privacy (lock, encryption, on-device)
  • Clean, stable interface
  • Daily recap / mood trend insight

Expected

  • Mood analysis & suggestions (AI readings, tags)
  • Multiple ways to log (text, photos, voice)

Reverse

  • Excessive reminders / forced pushes
  • Forced social binding
  • Dogmatic AI authoritative analysis
  • Complex input steps
  • Intrusive subscriptions & ads

Product goals

An effortless, pressure-free way to log moods

Log with the lowest possible friction — text, emoji, voice or photos — so recording becomes a daily habit, not a chore.

Immersive, sustainable healing

Sound, scenes, small tasks, interactive healing and a virtual companion give users relaxation, company and mental room to self-regulate and recover.

Reliable, unobtrusive mood insight

AI and data analysis offer gentle, non-judgmental readings and trend feedback — self-awareness without feeling judged or pushed.

Long-term companionship & growth

Gentle guidance, staged achievements and a journey-like growth path build self-understanding, rhythm and resilience — making mood management a lasting habit.

Design Strategy · 设计策略

“Start with pressure-free input, guide with gentle insight, heal through immersive care, and grow with lasting companionship — a healing app where emotions flow naturally.”

MOOD TRACE · 01

Auto mood tracking with the wristband

Once paired with the smart wristband, signals like HRV and resting heart rate stream in continuously. The app gauges your overall emotional state and nudges you to unwind when needed — no manual logging at all.

Manual logging to enrich mood details

A visual mood mascot

Visual mood data at a glance — the card also carries activity recommendations straight into healing content

MOOD TRACE · 02

Find the right way to unwind, right away

For your current state, the AI recommends fitting healing activities — or search directly, or browse categories like white noise and meditation.

Users can also search directly for activities that suit them

AI recommends activities for the current state, helping users quickly pick a way to unwind

Category filters let users jump straight to the healing format they want

MOOD TRACE · 03

Your emotional trajectory

Daily, weekly, and monthly views connect the dots: when you tend to dip, what's draining you — clear at a glance.

A reward mechanism that grants milestone rewards for taking part in healing activities

Scheduling: for people with bipolar disorder, managing daily life and taking medication on time is vital — this helps them manage themselves and live better
